# Outreach Policy

Follow these guardrails for every outreach task:

- Use only emails published by the business in public sources.
- Do not use purchased lists.
- Create drafts and daily send lists only; do not send email automatically.
- Keep initial Gmail volume low: up to 10 leads/day in wave 1 and up to 15 leads/day in wave 2.
- Include a clear opt-out line in every email.
- Never draft for a row marked with `baja` or `No contactar`.
- Do not attach files; use links to demos.
- Keep subjects honest and avoid pretending there was a previous conversation.
- Treat legal and deliverability guidance as operational guardrails, not legal advice.
